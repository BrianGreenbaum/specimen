Nabla was drawn by **Arthur Reinders Folmer** of Typearture, with font engineering by **Just van Rossum**, and joined Google Fonts in 2022. Its look comes out of isometric computer games: chunky blocks seen from above and to one side, lit from a single corner.

For decades a font file held one outline per letter, filled with a single colour. COLRv1 lets a glyph be a small composition instead — layers, gradients, a palette — and lets all of that *vary*. Nabla's extrusion is not an effect applied to letters; it is the letters, drawn all the way from flat to towering.
