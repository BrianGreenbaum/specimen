Handjet comes from **Rosetta**, the foundry of **David Březina**. A pixel font is normally frozen: someone decides the grid, fills in the squares, and that is the typeface. Handjet keeps the grid and unfreezes everything else.

Every glyph is built from copies of a single element, and the element is a variable: its shape, its size, and how many of it fill a unit are all axes you can set or animate. Johannes Neumeier, Borna Izadpanah, Khajag Apelian and Meir Sadan contributed to it, and it covers six scripts.
