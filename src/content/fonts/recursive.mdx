Recursive is led by **Stephen Nixon** at Arrow Type and came to Google Fonts in 2020. Its starting point is a trade rather than another typeface: *single-stroke casual*, the quick, warm brush lettering sign painters use for shop windows and price cards.

The family has two poles. **Linear** is crisp and regular, the voice of a well-kept terminal. **Casual** lets the brush back in. Both exist as a proportional Sans and a fixed-width Mono, and all of it sits in one file with every point between the poles available.

Across weight, the letters keep the same width. In an interface that means emphasis never nudges a layout; in code it means a bold keyword cannot knock a column out of line.
